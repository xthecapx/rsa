# Quantum Playground

**Your next adventure starts with a coin toss.**

Meet curious neighbors, explore hidden places, and build circuits to solve their
problems. A friendly coin toss leads to quantum searches, ghostly secrets, and a
cryptographic heist. Your experiments drive the adventure.

## A world to discover

- **Coin Town:** Help Ale and Brayan settle who goes first while discovering gates, superposition, and measurement.
- **Quantum Campus — optional:** Explore a science fair through predictions and experiments, from kets and superposition to entanglement and decoherence.
- **Foundry Town:** Harness interference and Grover’s algorithm to search sixteen hidden PINs in the Echo Chamber.
- **Hollow Town:** Question a mysterious oracle and use phase kickback to recover the 25-bit Ghost Key.
- **Cipher Town:** Earn gear, intercept messages, and progress from simple ciphers to breaking tiny demonstration RSA with Shor’s algorithm.
- **Quantum Town:** Return to your favorite challenges and revisit the ideas that brought you here.

## Make room for your own discoveries

Find hidden entrances to your personal cave lab. Arrange your space, unlock
equipment with challenge medals, and experiment freely with gates, X/Y/Z
rotations, Bloch spheres, and algorithm boxes.

## Before you set off

- **Controls:** Click/tap to walk, or use WASD/arrows; Space to talk. Touch controls are available.
- **Progress:** Neighbor lessons are optional; challenge medals open roads.
- **Experiments:** Press Run for actual measurements. Compare histograms with ideal references; consult the field notebook for help.
- **Checkpoints:** Before restarting or changing backends, keep your Save progress code. It restores milestones, not individual experiments.

Choose an IBM noise-model simulator for the full Qollab adventure. IonQ and
physical QPUs use local practice, reserving one remote execution per run for a
final challenge or lab experiment.

**Play in English or Spanish.**
